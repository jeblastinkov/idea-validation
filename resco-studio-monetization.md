# Resco Studio: Monetization Summary

**Goal:** sustainable recurring revenue, and attracting new customers into Studio so they also use Resco apps.
**Buyer:** customers (not partners). Studio sits next to Woodford, not in place of it.

## Recommended offers

| # | Offer | Pricing | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Studio plans with AI credits** | Free (small credit allowance) → Pro → Team → Enterprise. Monthly credits, then top-up packs. | The free tier attracts new customers. Credits cover AI cost. Plans give recurring revenue. |
| 2 | **Connection limits (MCP)** | Each plan includes N connected environments (Dataverse, Dynamics, Salesforce). Extra connections cost extra. Read-only could be free; write and publish paid. | Simple to explain and scales with customer size. |
| 3 | **Governance add-on** | Per-builder or per-environment add-on on Team and Enterprise. | Approvals, change history and rollback, dev → test → prod promotion, audit logs, limits on what AI may change. Enterprises pay for control over AI editing live apps. |
| 4 | **AI agents inside end-user apps** | Per user per month add-on on top of the existing 40–50 € app license, or an agent-action quota. | Largest revenue base. Examples: voice-to-record, photo inspection analysis, offline summaries. |

## Suggested order
1. Launch with offers 1 and 2.
2. Add offer 3 (governance).
3. Add offer 4 (end-user AI agents), with per-user usage caps to control AI cost.

## Attracting new customers
- Make the free tier enough to build and publish one small project.
- Make upgrading to paid Resco app licenses the natural next step.
- Show credit usage and limits before users hit them.

## Context (current Resco pricing, per public pricing page)
- Admin super user (Woodford): 250 €/user/month
- Manager: 50 €/user/month
- Field Sales+: from 40 €/user/month; Field Service+ and Inspections+: from 50 €/user/month
- Minimum 10 licenses

## Competitor benchmark (general knowledge, not live-verified)
- **Microsoft Power Apps / Copilot Studio:** AI bundled or sold as credits; sets price expectations. Resco edge: offline and deep field-service features.
- **Salesforce Agentforce:** per conversation or action; supports usage-based agent pricing.
- **Retool and similar AI builders:** builder seat plus AI usage; supports the tier model.

## Other ideas (backlog)
Paid MCP as a standalone product, marketplace (15–30% take rate), migration accelerator (Power Apps → Resco), questionnaire/forms AI, sync diagnostics premium tier, premium support and onboarding, training and certification, additional connector packs, embedded Studio API.

## Main risks
AI cost margins (pass through via credits), cannibalizing existing Woodford seats, Microsoft Copilot as a free alternative.
