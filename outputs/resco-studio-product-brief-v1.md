# Product Brief: Resco Studio

**Date**: 2026-05-19 | **Version**: 1.0 | **Status**: Draft

---

## Resco Studio: One platform. Every role. Your Resco — finally under control.

**For every person who touches a Resco deployment — the IT Manager building the app, the Field Service Manager designing the forms, the Admin keeping licenses in order — Resco Studio gives each of them a workspace built exactly for how they work.**

---

### The Villain

Managing a Resco deployment today means living in four places at once. The IT Manager has Woodford open in one tab, ChatGPT in another, a Questionnaire Designer window somewhere behind it, and a support ticket open with Resco because there's no self-service way to do what they need. The Field Service Manager emails a request to IT every time a form needs a new question. The Admin has no idea how many licenses are actually being used or which AI tool is running up the bill. Nobody has the full picture. Everything takes longer than it should.

---

### The Hero

Open Resco Studio and the right workspace appears for who you are. The IT Manager lands in Vibe Studio — a cloud IDE that already knows their entire Resco project: the data model, the workflows, every entity and relationship. They type "add a photo capture step to the work order form and hide it for read-only users" and it's done. The Field Service Manager opens Forms Studio and builds a 20-question safety inspection from a template in under ten minutes, no IT ticket required. The Admin sees every user, every license, every AI credit consumed — and can act on all of it without calling anyone.

---

### The Magic

**AI that knows your project.** Unlike ChatGPT, Resco Studio's AI has read your entire Resco setup before you type the first word. It doesn't need context. It doesn't make up field names. It modifies the real thing.

**Three roles, three workspaces, one login.** App Maker gets Vibe Studio (App Builder + Home Replacement + MCP Hub + Notes). Field Service Manager gets Forms Studio. IT Admin gets the Admin Portal. No role sees what isn't theirs.

**From description to deployed — no compilation.** Describe the UI component, the workflow change, or the new form. Resco Studio builds it, previews it, and applies it. No Xcode. No build pipeline. No waiting.

---

### Why Now?

Every major CRM vendor — Microsoft, Salesforce, ServiceNow — just launched an AI app builder. None of them knows how Resco works. As AI becomes the default way to build enterprise tools, Resco customers need an AI that speaks Resco natively — or they will start asking why their field service platform doesn't have one.

---

### The Evidence

> IT Managers at Resco customers currently use ChatGPT alongside Woodford to generate JavaScript customizations — a process requiring manual context injection every session, with no awareness of the live project structure. Resco Studio eliminates this entirely: the AI has direct access to the project, produces correct output on the first prompt, and applies changes without leaving the platform.
