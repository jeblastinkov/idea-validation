## Resco MCP Server: Your AI assistant finally knows Resco.

**For functional consultants building Resco mobile apps — so your AI builds, configures, and troubleshoots the entire app, not just snippets of code.**

---

### The Villain

You ask your AI assistant to generate Resco code, and it confidently writes something that looks right — but doesn't work. It's hallucinating. It has never seen the Resco JSBridge documentation and has no idea what tables or fields exist in your client's system. So you spend the next hour hunting through Resco Wikipedia, copying entity names by hand, and fixing code the AI got wrong. And that's before you even start configuring forms, views, and workflows — all of which still require clicking through the Woodford UI screen by screen, with no AI help at all. The tool that was supposed to save you time just doubled your work.

---

### The Hero

Now your AI assistant has direct access to the full Resco documentation and your client's live project — every entity, field, form, view, workflow, and sync rule. You describe what you need in plain language, and the AI builds it. Add a field to a form. Set up a sync filter. Create a workflow that fires on save. Reorder the home screen navigation. All from a single conversation, with no manual clicking. When a sync issue hits, you don't dig through logs alone. You ask your AI, and it pinpoints the problem in seconds.

---

### The Magic

**Accurate code, first try.** Your AI reads the complete Resco JSBridge documentation, so it stops guessing and starts generating code that actually runs.

**Your data, your context.** The AI sees your client's exact tables, fields, and entity configuration — no more manual lookups or copy-paste errors.

**Build forms and views without clicking.** Add fields, tabs, and buttons to forms. Configure view columns, filters, and sort order. All through natural language — the AI writes directly to the project.

**Automate workflows instantly.** Create, validate, and deploy business logic on forms and views. The AI generates workflow XML, validates syntax, and applies changes — without you touching the workflow editor.

**Navigation and theming, done right.** Customize the home screen, manage navigation groups, and inspect app themes — all through conversation.

**Sync issues, solved fast.** Point the AI at a sync log and get a clear explanation of what went wrong and how to fix it, instead of hours of troubleshooting.
